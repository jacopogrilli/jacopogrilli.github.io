Examples:
Species 1, species 2. Densities in units of the carrying capacities, ui = Ni/Ki; time in units of 1/r1. First panel: a12 = α12K2/K1, a21 = α21K1/K2; the quadrants are the four outcomes. Phase plane: coloured lines are the nullclines, u1 + a12u2 = 1 and a21u1 + u2 = 1 (the axes are nullclines too); grey arrows the direction of the flow; blue line the trajectory; dashed black the separatrix. Black dots: fixed points, filled if stable, open if unstable; blue open circle: the start. Last panel: u1(t) and u2(t), from that start. The ratio r2/r1 changes the trajectories and the separatrix, not the nullclines, the fixed points or the outcome.
