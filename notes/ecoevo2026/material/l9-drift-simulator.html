Model: Examples:
populations of N haploid individuals, all starting from the same frequency x0 of type A. Moran: each individual reproduces at rate 1 + s (A) or 1 (a), and its offspring replaces an individual chosen at random; one generation is N replacements. Wright–Fisher: all N offspring are drawn at once from the previous generation. In both, an offspring mutates to the other type with probability μ. Grey lines: single populations (one in blue). Histogram: the blue bars at the ends are the populations where A is lost (x = 0) or fixed (x = 1). Heterozygosity: black, average over the populations; dashed blue, the exact result for s = 0. Last panel: fixed and lost; dashed lines, the predicted fixation probability and its complement, for μ = 0.
