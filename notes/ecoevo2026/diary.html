Tue 22/9 — Logistics and exam, then the shape of the whole course: what we will write, and what it leaves out. We built exponential growth from the simplest scenario, birth and death, saw the same thing in discrete generations, and put numbers on it — orders of magnitude and what fraction of all humans ever born is alive today. The rest of the class was about why identical cultures do not grow identically: the observation, what a rate is, and the fact that the mean obeys the deterministic equation. We wrote the master equation, and of the probability generating function only the idea, not the derivation.
